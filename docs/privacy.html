Privacy policy

Space Race 2.0 · Last updated 29 October 2026

In short

Space Race 2.0 does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Data the app loads

To show launches, crews, station positions and statistics, the app downloads public information from these services:

  • Launch Library 2 by The Space Devs (ll.thespacedevs.com): launches and crews
  • CelesTrak (celestrak.org): orbital data for the ISS and Tiangong
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com): daily launch statistics for this app

As with any internet request, these services receive your device’s IP address and basic technical information to deliver the data. The app sends them nothing else. Their own privacy policies apply to their servers.

Data on your device

The app keeps a copy of the downloaded data and your launch reminders on your device, so it works offline and does not reload everything each time. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Notifications

If you ask for a launch reminder, the app asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no push service or server is involved.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
